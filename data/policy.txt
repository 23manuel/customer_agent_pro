1.0 Purpose

This Policy establishes the operational standards, customer protection requirements, data privacy obligations, and credit administration procedures applicable to NovaPay Financial Services (“NovaPay”).

The objective of this document is to:
	•	Ensure consistent and fair treatment of customers;
	•	Define service delivery expectations and escalation procedures;
	•	Protect customer data and maintain regulatory compliance;
	•	Establish responsible lending standards;
	•	Reduce operational, legal, and reputational risk.

This Policy applies to all NovaPay employees, contractors, third-party service providers, customer support personnel, and business partners involved in customer servicing, data handling, payments, savings products, and lending operations.

⸻

2.0 Scope

This Policy applies to:
	•	Mobile payment services;
	•	Digital wallet accounts;
	•	Savings accounts;
	•	Micro-loan products;
	•	Customer support operations;
	•	Data collection and processing activities;
	•	Third-party integrations and vendors;
	•	Recovery and collections activities.

This Policy covers all customers, including individual users and small-to-medium enterprises (SMEs) operating on the NovaPay platform.

⸻

3.0 Definitions

3.1 Customer

Any individual or registered business using a NovaPay product or service.

3.2 Business Day

Any day excluding weekends and officially recognized public holidays in the jurisdiction where the customer account is registered.

3.3 Personal Data

Any information capable of identifying an individual directly or indirectly, including but not limited to name, phone number, identification number, email address, transaction records, and device identifiers.

3.4 Refund

A reversal of funds to a customer following a failed, duplicate, unauthorized, or disputed transaction.

3.5 Delinquent Account

A loan account that remains unpaid beyond the scheduled repayment date.

⸻

4.0 Customer Support & Refunds Policy

4.1 Customer Support Principles

NovaPay shall maintain a customer support framework that is accessible, timely, transparent, and compliant with applicable consumer protection standards.

Support services shall be available through:
	•	In-app live chat;
	•	Email support;
	•	Telephone support;
	•	Social media support channels;
	•	Automated chatbot assistance.

Support operations shall function 24 hours per day for critical payment incidents and from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM local time for standard customer inquiries.

⸻

4.2 Response Time Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

4.2.1 First Response Time

Inquiry Type	Target First Response
Payment failure	Within 15 minutes
Unauthorized transaction	Within 10 minutes
Loan inquiry	Within 1 hour
Savings account inquiry	Within 2 hours
General support inquiry	Within 4 hours
Email requests	Within 8 business hours

Automated acknowledgements generated by chatbots or ticketing systems shall not constitute a completed first response unless accompanied by a ticket reference number and estimated resolution timeline.

⸻

4.2.2 Resolution Time Targets

Issue Type	Target Resolution Time
Failed transfer reversal	Within 24 hours
Duplicate debit complaint	Within 48 hours
Unauthorized transaction investigation	Within 5 business days
Refund processing	Within 5–10 business days
Loan repayment disputes	Within 7 business days
Account verification issues	Within 3 business days

Cases involving law enforcement, fraud investigations, court orders, or third-party banking partners may require extended resolution timelines.

⸻

4.3 Escalation Framework

NovaPay shall maintain a four-level escalation structure to ensure prompt handling of customer complaints.

4.3.1 Level 1 – Automated Support
	•	Chatbot handles routine inquiries;
	•	Password reset guidance;
	•	Transaction status checks;
	•	FAQ-based responses.

Cases unresolved after 10 minutes or two failed automated interactions shall automatically escalate to a human support agent.

⸻

4.3.2 Level 2 – Customer Support Agent

Support agents are authorized to:
	•	Investigate transaction history;
	•	Verify account activity;
	•	Initiate standard refunds below USD 250 equivalent;
	•	Resolve common account issues.

Escalation to Level 3 shall occur where:
	•	Fraud indicators exist;
	•	Customer dissatisfaction persists;
	•	Financial exposure exceeds USD 250;
	•	Regulatory risk is identified.

⸻

4.3.3 Level 3 – Senior Resolution Team

The Senior Resolution Team shall handle:
	•	Complex disputes;
	•	Fraud investigations;
	•	Chargeback disputes;
	•	Escalated SME complaints;
	•	Repeat complaints within a 90-day period.

Senior officers may authorize goodwill compensation up to USD 500 equivalent where operational negligence is confirmed.

⸻

4.3.4 Level 4 – Legal & Compliance

The Legal & Compliance Department shall manage:
	•	Regulatory complaints;
	•	Data privacy breaches;
	•	Court orders and subpoenas;
	•	Suspicious transaction investigations;
	•	Threatened or actual litigation.

All escalations to Legal & Compliance must include complete interaction logs and supporting evidence.

⸻

4.4 Refund Eligibility Policy

Customers may qualify for refunds under the following circumstances:

4.4.1 Eligible Refund Scenarios
	•	Duplicate transactions;
	•	Failed transactions where customer account was debited;
	•	Unauthorized or fraudulent transactions;
	•	Merchant non-delivery disputes;
	•	Technical system errors attributable to NovaPay;
	•	Incorrect account debits caused by operational error.

⸻

4.4.2 Non-Eligible Refund Scenarios

Refunds shall generally not be approved where:
	•	The customer authorized the transaction;
	•	Incorrect recipient details were entered by the customer;
	•	Loan disbursement funds have already been utilized;
	•	Merchant disputes fall outside the allowable dispute period;
	•	Transactions involve prohibited or illegal activity.

⸻

4.5 Refund Processing Timelines

Refund Type	Processing Timeline
Wallet reversal	24–48 hours
Bank transfer reversal	3–5 business days
Card-based refund	5–10 business days
Merchant dispute refund	Up to 15 business days

Refund approvals exceeding USD 1,000 equivalent require dual authorization from Compliance and Finance.

⸻

4.6 Dispute Resolution Process

4.6.1 Complaint Submission

Customers may submit disputes through:
	•	In-app support center;
	•	Official support email;
	•	Telephone hotline;
	•	Written complaint submission.

All complaints must include:
	•	Transaction reference number;
	•	Date and time of transaction;
	•	Description of issue;
	•	Supporting evidence where applicable.

⸻

4.6.2 Investigation Process

NovaPay shall:
	1.	Acknowledge the complaint;
	2.	Assign a case reference number;
	3.	Conduct transaction review;
	4.	Request additional evidence if required;
	5.	Provide investigation findings;
	6.	Communicate resolution outcome.

⸻

4.6.3 External Resolution

Where internal resolution fails, customers may escalate matters to:
	•	Applicable financial ombudsman services;
	•	Consumer protection authorities;
	•	Central banking regulators;
	•	Courts of competent jurisdiction.

NovaPay shall cooperate fully with lawful investigations and regulatory inquiries.

⸻

5.0 Data Privacy & Usage Policy

5.1 Privacy Commitment

NovaPay is committed to protecting customer data and maintaining confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets.

All data processing activities shall comply with applicable data protection laws in jurisdictions where NovaPay operates.

⸻

5.2 Data Collection

NovaPay collects data necessary for service delivery, fraud prevention, regulatory compliance, and product improvement.

5.2.1 Personal Information Collected

The following categories of information may be collected:
	•	Full legal name;
	•	Date of birth;
	•	Residential address;
	•	Government-issued identification;
	•	Phone number;
	•	Email address;
	•	Biometric verification data;
	•	Selfie photographs;
	•	Employment and income information;
	•	Banking and transaction records.

⸻

5.2.2 Technical and Device Information

NovaPay may collect:
	•	Device identifiers;
	•	IP addresses;
	•	Browser type;
	•	Geolocation data;
	•	App usage logs;
	•	Cookies and analytics data.

⸻

5.3 Purpose of Data Collection

Customer data may be used for:
	•	Identity verification;
	•	Transaction processing;
	•	Loan underwriting;
	•	Fraud monitoring;
	•	Customer support;
	•	Regulatory reporting;
	•	Product analytics;
	•	Service improvement;
	•	Marketing communications where consent is obtained.

NovaPay shall not sell customer personal data to third parties.

⸻

5.4 Lawful Basis for Processing

Data processing shall occur based on one or more of the following legal grounds:
	•	Customer consent;
	•	Contractual necessity;
	•	Legal and regulatory obligations;
	•	Legitimate business interests;
	•	Fraud prevention and security protection.

⸻

5.5 Data Storage and Security

5.5.1 Storage Standards

Customer data shall be stored using encrypted databases hosted within approved cloud infrastructure environments.

Sensitive customer information shall be encrypted:
	•	In transit using TLS 1.2 or higher;
	•	At rest using AES-256 encryption standards.

⸻

5.5.2 Access Controls

NovaPay shall implement:
	•	Multi-factor authentication (MFA);
	•	Role-based access controls;
	•	Privileged access monitoring;
	•	Session timeout policies;
	•	Employee access reviews every quarter.

Employees shall only access customer information strictly necessary for job performance.

⸻

5.5.3 Security Monitoring

NovaPay shall maintain:
	•	24/7 fraud monitoring systems;
	•	Intrusion detection systems;
	•	Endpoint protection tools;
	•	Security event logging;
	•	Penetration testing at least twice annually.

Critical vulnerabilities identified through security assessments must be remediated within 14 calendar days.

⸻

5.6 Data Retention Policy

Data Type	Retention Period
Customer account records	7 years after closure
Transaction records	10 years
Loan agreements	10 years
Customer support interactions	3 years
Marketing consent records	5 years
Fraud investigation records	10 years

Data may be retained beyond standard retention periods where required by law, court order, or ongoing investigation.

⸻

5.7 Customer Rights

Customers may exercise the following rights subject to applicable law:

5.7.1 Right of Access

Customers may request copies of personal data held by NovaPay.

NovaPay shall respond within 30 calendar days.

⸻

5.7.2 Right to Correction

Customers may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.

Corrections shall be processed within 14 business days after verification.

⸻

5.7.3 Right to Deletion

Customers may request deletion of personal data where:
	•	Data is no longer required;
	•	Consent has been withdrawn;
	•	Processing is unlawful.

Deletion requests may be denied where data retention is legally required.

⸻

5.7.4 Right to Restrict Processing

Customers may request temporary suspension of certain processing activities during dispute investigations.

⸻

5.8 Third-Party Data Sharing

NovaPay may share customer data only with approved third parties for legitimate operational purposes.

5.8.1 Approved Third Parties
	•	Banking partners;
	•	Payment processors;
	•	Identity verification providers;
	•	Credit bureaus;
	•	Fraud prevention vendors;
	•	Regulatory authorities;
	•	Cloud hosting providers.

⸻

5.8.2 Vendor Requirements

All third-party vendors must:
	•	Sign confidentiality agreements;
	•	Meet NovaPay security standards;
	•	Undergo annual vendor risk assessments;
	•	Maintain adequate cybersecurity controls.

Third parties shall not use customer data for unauthorized marketing or resale purposes.

⸻

5.9 Cross-Border Data Transfers

Cross-border data transfers shall only occur where:
	•	Adequate data protection safeguards exist;
	•	Contractual protections are implemented;
	•	Regulatory requirements are satisfied.

Sensitive customer information shall not be transferred to jurisdictions subject to sanctions or elevated cybersecurity risk without Compliance approval.

⸻

5.10 Data Breach Notification Policy

5.10.1 Incident Reporting

Employees must report suspected data breaches within one hour of discovery to:
	•	Information Security Team;
	•	Compliance Department;
	•	Chief Technology Officer.

⸻

5.10.2 Investigation Timeline

NovaPay shall:
	•	Initiate breach assessment within 4 hours;
	•	Contain identified threats immediately;
	•	Conduct forensic investigation;
	•	Document impact and remediation actions.

⸻

5.10.3 Customer Notification

Affected customers shall be notified without unreasonable delay where the breach presents significant risk of:
	•	Financial fraud;
	•	Identity theft;
	•	Unauthorized account access;
	•	Exposure of sensitive personal information.

Where legally required, regulators shall be notified within 72 hours of confirmed breach discovery.

⸻

6.0 Loan & Credit Terms Policy

6.1 Lending Principles

NovaPay is committed to responsible lending practices that promote financial inclusion while minimizing customer over-indebtedness.

Loan approvals shall be based on affordability, repayment history, and risk assessment outcomes.

⸻

6.2 Loan Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a NovaPay loan product, applicants must:
	•	Be at least 18 years old;
	•	Possess a valid government-issued ID;
	•	Maintain an active NovaPay account for at least 30 days;
	•	Complete Know Your Customer (KYC) verification;
	•	Demonstrate consistent transaction activity;
	•	Meet minimum internal credit score thresholds.

SMEs applying for business loans must additionally provide:
	•	Business registration details;
	•	Trading history of at least 6 months;
	•	Business bank statements where applicable.

⸻

6.3 Loan Limits

Customer Segment	Minimum Loan	Maximum Loan
Individual users	USD 20	USD 2,000
SMEs	USD 100	USD 10,000

Loan limits may increase based on repayment performance and account history.

⸻

6.4 Interest Rate Structure

6.4.1 Standard Interest Rates

Loan Product	Monthly Interest Rate
Personal micro-loan	3.5% – 7.5%
SME working capital loan	2.8% – 6.0%
Salary advance loan	2.5% flat

Interest rates are determined based on:
	•	Internal credit score;
	•	Transaction history;
	•	Repayment behavior;
	•	Existing debt exposure;
	•	Fraud risk indicators.

⸻

6.4.2 Fees and Charges

Fee Type	Amount
Loan processing fee	1.5% of principal
Early repayment fee	None
Late payment fee	USD 5 equivalent
Failed direct debit fee	USD 2 equivalent

All applicable fees must be disclosed prior to loan acceptance.

⸻

6.5 Loan Disbursement

Approved loans shall generally be disbursed instantly into the customer’s NovaPay wallet.

Additional verification may delay disbursement for:
	•	High-value loans;
	•	Newly registered accounts;
	•	Suspicious activity flags.

⸻

6.6 Repayment Schedules

6.6.1 Standard Loan Terms

Loan Type	Repayment Duration
Short-term micro-loan	14–90 days
SME loan	3–12 months
Salary advance	Next salary cycle

Repayment frequency may be:
	•	Daily;
	•	Weekly;
	•	Bi-weekly;
	•	Monthly.

⸻

6.6.2 Grace Period

NovaPay provides:
	•	A 3-calendar-day grace period for individual loans;
	•	A 5-calendar-day grace period for SME loans.

Interest shall continue to accrue during the grace period unless otherwise specified.

⸻

6.7 Late Payment Policy

Accounts shall be classified as overdue immediately after expiration of the applicable grace period.

6.7.1 Penalties

The following may apply:
	•	Late payment fee;
	•	Temporary account restrictions;
	•	Reduction of future loan eligibility;
	•	Reporting to credit bureaus.

Late fees shall not exceed 10% of the outstanding principal balance.

⸻

6.8 Collections and Recovery Process

6.8.1 Early Delinquency (1–30 Days Past Due)

NovaPay shall initiate:
	•	SMS reminders;
	•	Push notifications;
	•	Email reminders;
	•	Automated payment prompts.

⸻

6.8.2 Mid-Stage Delinquency (31–60 Days Past Due)

Actions may include:
	•	Direct customer outreach;
	•	Structured repayment negotiation;
	•	Temporary suspension of account features;
	•	Reporting to licensed credit bureaus.

⸻

6.8.3 Severe Delinquency (61+ Days Past Due)

NovaPay may:
	•	Transfer account to internal recovery unit;
	•	Engage approved collections agencies;
	•	Commence legal recovery proceedings;
	•	Offset available wallet balances where legally permitted.

Collections activities must remain respectful, lawful, and non-harassing at all times.

⸻

6.9 Default Policy

A loan shall be considered in default after 90 consecutive days of non-payment.

Defaulted accounts may result in:
	•	Permanent reduction of credit access;
	•	Legal recovery action;
	•	Credit bureau reporting;
	•	Account suspension.

NovaPay shall maintain records of all recovery efforts and customer communications.

⸻

6.10 Hardship Assistance

Customers experiencing genuine financial hardship may apply for temporary repayment relief measures including:
	•	Payment restructuring;
	•	Extended repayment terms;
	•	Reduced installment plans;
	•	Temporary payment suspension.

Hardship accommodations require documented review and management approval.

⸻

7.0 Employee Responsibilities

All employees are responsible for:
	•	Adhering to this Policy;
	•	Protecting confidential information;
	•	Escalating suspicious activity;
	•	Completing mandatory compliance training annually;
	•	Reporting policy breaches immediately.

Failure to comply with this Policy may result in disciplinary action, including termination of employment.

⸻

8.0 Compliance Monitoring

NovaPay shall conduct periodic reviews of:
	•	Customer complaint handling;
	•	Refund turnaround times;
	•	Data access logs;
	•	Loan portfolio performance;
	•	Vendor compliance;
	•	Security controls.

Internal audits shall be conducted at least annually.

⸻

9.0 Policy Exceptions

Any exception to this Policy requires:
	•	Written justification;
	•	Approval from the Head of Compliance;
	•	Risk assessment documentation;
	•	Executive management authorization where material risk exists.
